Couchcast

Movie night, no matter where everyone is.

One friend hosts and plays a movie from their computer. Everyone else joins with a code or link and watches in sync, with voice chat, reactions and subtitles. Video goes straight between your devices: no account, no upload, free.

    Connection settings (if a friend can't connect)

    Some networks (Jio, Airtel, mobile hotspots, office Wi-Fi) block direct connections between two devices. A relay server fixes this. Enter it here on both the host and friend devices, or put it in the site once so everyone gets it.
